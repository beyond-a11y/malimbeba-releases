Malimbe by Beyond-A11y

Privacy policy

Applies to the Malimbe MCP server, its plugins and its Skills. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Summary

Malimbe runs on your computer. It has no account, no sign-in and no telemetry. Beyond-A11y, LLC does not receive your code, your prompts, the pages you audit or your audit results.

What Malimbe stores, and where

Malimbe keeps these files on your computer, in a .malimbeba folder in your user profile or your project:

  • The date your trial started.
  • Your paid validation code, if you add one. It is checked on your computer; it is not sent anywhere.
  • Audit evidence files and the component manifest, when you use the tools that create them.

Delete the folder to remove them.

What Malimbe sends over the network

  • Pages you ask it to audit. Malimbe requests the URLs you give it, the way a browser would. Those sites, and any third-party services they load, see a normal page visit from your network address.
  • Browser download, only if you turn it on. If you run the automatic browser install, the browser is downloaded from Playwright's servers.

Malimbe makes no other network requests.

Your AI assistant

Malimbe returns its results to the AI app you run it in, such as Claude, GitHub Copilot or Cursor. What that app does with your prompts and results is covered by its own privacy policy, not this one.

Installing and buying

Downloads come from GitHub or npm, which keep their own logs. Purchases are made on beyond-a11y.com and are covered by that site's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
